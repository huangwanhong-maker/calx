# Release Notes: 4 November 2026

## 1. The Symmetric 3 × 3 Eigenproblem in Closed Form

In this update calx gives the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a symmetric 3 × 3 matrix in closed form, fast enough for one problem at every voxel of a volume ([decision 0015](../decisions/0015-the-symmetric-three-by-three-eigenproblem.md); the infrastructure's [ADR 0070](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0070-structures-reconstructed-from-images.md)).

- **Accurate everywhere.** The trigonometric roots of the characteristic cubic serve where the eigenvalues are apart. Where two nearly coincide, as a vessel's two cross-sectional curvatures do, the cyclic Jacobi method finishes the problem, so the eigenvalues are right to a few ulp of the matrix's norm. The eigenvectors always form an orthonormal, right-handed frame.
- **Checked against the general solver.** On 10,000 random matrices the eigenvalues agree with Householder–QL to 10⁻¹³ of the norm. Double eigenvalues are exact to rounding, and a tube's Hessian gives its axis.
- **First use.** The new image foundation eikon reads tubes, blobs and sheets from the Hessian's eigenvalues at every voxel, to reconstruct vessels from CT and MR.
