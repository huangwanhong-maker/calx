# Release Notes: 18 November 2026

## Greedy Choice with a Bound on the Best

`optimisation` can now choose a set greedily and say how far from the best it can be ([decision 0019](../decisions/0019-greedy-submodular.md)).

- **Under a number:** the greedy choice of a monotone submodular function, with lazy evaluation, at least 1 − 1/e of the best, and a bound on the best computed from the instance, usually far tighter.
- **Under a budget:** the better of greedy by gain and by gain per cost, at least (1 − 1/e)/2 of the best, with its bound.
