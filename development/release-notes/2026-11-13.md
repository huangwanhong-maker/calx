# Release Notes: 13 November 2026

## 1. The Leading Walks of a Neumann Series

In this update calx can say which paths carry most of an inverse (I − Z)⁻¹ y, when Z is non-negative and its spectral radius is below one ([decision 0017](../decisions/0017-the-leading-walks-of-a-neumann-series.md)). In input–output economics this is structural path analysis: which chains of suppliers carry most of a product's emissions. This serves the infrastructure's industrial ecology domain ([ADR 0106](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/Noetarium-The-Science-and-Humanity-Infrastructure/blob/main/development/decisions/0106-industrial-ecology-founded.md)).

- **The walks, largest first,** down to a cutoff one states, to a depth and within a budget of visits.
- **An exact bound on what is left.** A branch is cut only when its weight times everything that can follow it falls below the cutoff, so no walk above the cutoff is missed. The answer says how much of the whole the walks visited cover, and how much lies at each depth.
- **Checks.** A chain without cycles is covered exactly; the series by depth sums to the whole; the search finds every walk above the cutoff that brute force finds to depth six; negative entries are refused.

## 2. Non-Negative Matrices

In this update calx answers what is asked of a non-negative matrix: a population counted by stages, a Markov chain, a next-generation operator. This serves the infrastructure's ecology domain ([ADR 0101](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/Noetarium-The-Science-and-Humanity-Infrastructure/blob/main/development/decisions/0101-ecology-founded-the-urban-forest.md)).

- **The Perron root and its vectors** for any non-negative matrix: primitive, imprimitive or reducible. λ comes from the QR algorithm, the vectors from inverse iteration, so a matrix whose powers oscillate is served too. The answer also says whether the matrix is irreducible, its period, its damping ratio, and whether λ is simple.
- **What λ answers to:** its sensitivity to every entry, and the elasticities, which sum to one.
- **A process that leaves its states:** the fundamental matrix, the expected time before leaving from each state, and R₀, the members a member leaves over its life.
- **A repair.** The eigenvalues of the zero matrix were never returned: the QR iteration did not stop. They now come at once. A population that makes no new members, such as planted street trees, has such a next-generation matrix.
