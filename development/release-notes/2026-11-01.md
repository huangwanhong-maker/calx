# Release Notes: 1 November 2026

## 1. Assignment, and Multinomial Draws

In this update calx can solve assignment problems and draw multinomial counts ([decision 0014](../decisions/0014-assignment-and-multinomial-variates.md); the infrastructure's [ADR 0064](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0064-abnormal-genomes-their-description-and-expression.md)).

- **The Hungarian method.** It pairs two sets at least cost, or at greatest similarity. It returns its dual potentials as a certificate that the pairing is optimal. The genomics domain uses it to match the mutational signatures it extracts with the known ones.
- **Multinomial variates.** Drawn by conditional binomials, from a keyed random stream. These are the resamplings of a mutational catalogue for bootstrap intervals and for the stability of factorisations.
