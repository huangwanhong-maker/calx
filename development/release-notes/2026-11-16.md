# Release Notes: 16 November 2026

## 1. The Knapsack, with a Certificate

In this update calx chooses the subset of greatest value within a capacity: the 0/1 knapsack ([decision 0018](../decisions/0018-the-knapsack.md)). It serves tyche's choice of which tests to run before several decisions within one budget, for the infrastructure's evidence in space and time ([ADR 0123](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/Noetarium-The-Science-and-Humanity-Infrastructure/blob/main/development/decisions/0123-evidence-in-space-and-time.md)).

- **Branch and bound,** depth first in the order of value per weight, each node bounded by Dantzig's relaxation. Its first leaf is the greedy answer.
- **A certificate.** When the search ends, the answer is exact. When it is stopped at a limit of nodes, the best found comes back with an upper bound, so the optimum is known to lie between the two.
- **Checks.** Every subset of small random instances is enumerated and the optimum matched; items of no value, of no weight and heavier than the capacity are handled as stated; a stopped search's bound holds.
