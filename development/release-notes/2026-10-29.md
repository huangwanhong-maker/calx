# Release Notes: 29 October 2026

## 1. The Matrices and Minima of Statistics; the θ-Method; Symplectic Integrators and Small Systems

In this update calx gains what the inference foundation computes with, an integrator for fields that evolve, and integrators that keep what mechanics conserves ([decision 0008](../decisions/0008-optimisation-and-least-squares.md), [decision 0011](../decisions/0011-the-theta-method.md), [decision 0012](../decisions/0012-symplectic-integrators-and-small-systems.md)).

- **The matrices and minima of statistics.** Rectangular matrices, QR with column pivoting and least squares, Cholesky and band Cholesky factorisations, and the eigenvalues of general matrices. Minima of several variables by Nelder–Mead and by BFGS, with derivatives by differences when none are given, and linear programmes by the simplex method. These are what tyche's regressions and time series, and nomos's econometrics, compute with.
- **The θ-method.** One step of a large stiff linear system, explicit Euler, Crank–Nicolson or implicit Euler by the choice of θ, solved by conjugate gradients. physis's heat conduction steps with it.
- **Symplectic integrators.** Störmer–Verlet, whose energy error stays bounded over a thousand orbits instead of drifting, and RATTLE, which holds a body to a surface to rounding and returns the surface's force on it: the normal force a rover's wheels push against.
- **Small systems.** A solve in a few unknowns without allocation, for the Newton steps of implicit methods.

The checks hold them to closed forms, published examples and the dense solve.

## 2. The Kernel's Real-Time Classes, Asked; Legendre Functions in Extended Range

In this update calx asks the operating system what it grants a scheduler, and its Legendre functions hold at every latitude to very high degree ([decision 0010](../decisions/0010-kernel-awareness-and-extended-range-legendre.md)).

- **Asking the kernel.** A program can now learn whether Linux would give its threads a real-time class: SCHED_FIFO with its range of priorities, or SCHED_DEADLINE. The report says whether the kernel is fully preemptible and whether it runs in WSL, and why a class is refused. Each class is tried on a short-lived thread and given back, so asking changes nothing.
- **Raising the workers.** A pool's threads can be raised to SCHED_FIFO when the kernel allows it, and lowered again; Cosmopoiesis offers this as a choice of its acquisition. On WSL 2 both classes are refused, and the report says so.
- **Legendre functions near the poles.** Schmidt's functions now keep each order in extended range, so the values that fall below the smallest double near the poles at high degree are no longer lost. They hold from the equator to within a few millionths of a radian of each pole up to degree 2700, which the Moon's crustal field of degree 449 needs.

The checks hold that the probe leaves the caller as it was, that a raise succeeds exactly when the probe says it would, that every refusal has a reason, that the addition theorem holds to the rounding error analysis predicts at degrees 449, 1000 and 2700, and that the derivatives agree with central differences near the poles.
