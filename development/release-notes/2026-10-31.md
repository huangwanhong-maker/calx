# Release Notes: 31 October 2026

## 1. Multitaper Spectra and Bispectra, and a Faster QR

In this update calx estimates spectra as a geophysicist does, for the rhythm of streets ([decision 0013](../decisions/0013-multitaper-spectra-and-bispectra.md); the infrastructure's [ADR 0060](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0060-streets-as-open-systems.md)).

- **Slepian tapers.** The discrete prolate spheroidal sequences of any length and time–bandwidth, with their concentrations, held to SciPy's.
- **Thomson's multitaper estimate**, with adaptive weights and its degrees of freedom; the tapered periodogram; cross spectra and coherence; the bispectrum and the bicoherence of a series, quadratic phase coupling found at its pair of frequencies.
- **A faster QR.** Householder's reflections are applied row by row, as a matrix is stored, rather than column by column. Each sum keeps its order, so the factors are the same to the bit (a new check holds them so), and a least-squares fit of 17,000 hourly counts at 109 columns takes 0.27 s instead of 1.2 s. Every caller gains: tyche's harmonic analysis and regressions among them.
