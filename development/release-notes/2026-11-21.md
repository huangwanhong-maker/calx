# Release Notes: 21 November 2026

## Band Matrices That Are Not Symmetric

`linalg` can now solve a general band matrix in time proportional to its order ([decision 0020](../decisions/0020-band-lu.md)): a nonsymmetric system with a few diagonals either side, such as the generator of a density moved by drift and diffusion on a grid of cells, is factorised by Gaussian elimination with partial pivoting within the band and solved without ever forming the dense matrix.
